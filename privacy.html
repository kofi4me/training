Learner Data and Account Records

Account and Assessment Records

KryptNet stores the name, verified email address, and account identifier supplied by its sign-in provider. The platform records successful sign-ins, observed authenticated visits, first course openings, assessed completions, and issued certificate identifiers. The most recent 100 account activity entries are retained in each account record; course outcomes are stored separately. Opening a course is not proof of participation or mastery. Anonymous visitors are not identified by this account system.

Assessment registration, responses, scores, and certificate files are stored by the server. Optional organization and learner identifiers remain in the protected registration record. An issuer administrator can review account and course records. Public certificate verification discloses the certificate holder's name, course, score, completion date, and PDF fingerprint to anyone with the certificate identifier. Confirm your registration details only after understanding this disclosure.

Browser Storage and Sign-in

The sign-in provider handles authentication and has its own privacy terms. The platform does not receive or store your password. An essential HTTP-only session cookie lasts up to eight hours. Signing out ends the local application session, not necessarily the provider's own session. Lab configurations, messages, notes, and practice markers remain in browser storage and are not account-synchronised course evidence. Avoid shared browsers for personal work and export or clear local work before leaving a shared device.

Operator Responsibilities

Before public enrolment, KryptNet must publish its applicable privacy notice, data-retention schedule, support contact, and procedures for access, correction, and deletion requests. This technical description does not replace that notice. Hosting logs, identity-provider records, and backups must be included in those procedures. This deployment has not yet completed that operational review.
