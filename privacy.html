Learner Data and Account Records

Account and Assessment Records

KryptNet stores your name, verified email address, account identifier, and optional organization. For email registration, KryptNet sends a temporary confirmation code to the address you provide. The platform records successful sign-ins, observed authenticated visits, first course openings, assessed completions, and issued certificate identifiers. The most recent 100 account activity entries are retained in each account record; course outcomes are stored separately. Opening a course is not proof of participation or mastery. Anonymous visitors are not identified by this account system.

Assessment registration, responses, scores, and certificate files are stored by the server. Optional organization and learner identifiers remain in the protected registration record. An issuer administrator can review account and course records. Public certificate verification discloses the certificate holder's name, course, score, completion date, and PDF fingerprint to anyone with the certificate identifier. Confirm your registration details only after understanding this disclosure.

Browser Storage and Sign-in

KryptNet stores a salted password hash for accounts created with email and password. Provider sign-in is handled under that provider's privacy terms. Authorize.Net hosts card checkout. KryptNet stores the order and payment confirmation for each paid assessment. An essential HTTP-only session cookie lasts up to eight hours. Signing out ends the local application session, not necessarily the provider's own session. Lab configurations, messages, notes, and practice markers remain in browser storage and are not account-synchronised course evidence. Avoid shared browsers for personal work and export or clear local work before leaving a shared device.

Operator Responsibilities

Before public enrolment, KryptNet must publish its applicable privacy notice, data-retention schedule, support contact, and procedures for access, correction, and deletion requests. This technical description does not replace that notice. Hosting logs, identity-provider records, and backups must be included in those procedures. This deployment has not yet completed that operational review.
