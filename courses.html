How It Works

  1. Choose Your Course

    Browse our training categories and select a course that matches your interests, experience, or professional development goals.

  2. Learn Through Guided Tutorials

    Build your understanding through structured lessons, technical explanations, demonstrations, and practical examples.

  3. Practice in Interactive Labs

    Apply your knowledge in realistic virtual environments. Configure systems, troubleshoot problems, investigate incidents, and verify your results through practical lab exercises.

  4. Complete Your Assessment

    After completing your training, register or sign in to take the optional online assessment. Review the assessment requirements and pay the applicable fee when ready.

  5. Earn Your Certificate

    Successfully complete the assessment and practical requirements to receive your downloadable KryptNet Certificate of Completion.
