Pilot procedure and acceptance criteria
  1. Obtain consent and explain that the product, not the participant, is being evaluated.
  2. Ask the participant to perform each task without coaching. Record confusion, requests for help, and incorrect interpretations.
  3. Start the timer for a task, observe the attempt, then record whether it was completed unaided, with help, or not completed.
  4. Run at least three beginner sessions before the first pilot decision. Investigate every incorrect causal explanation or blocked task; repeat affected tasks with new learners after fixes.

A release decision requires no unresolved critical learning errors, successful unaided completion of essential tasks, understandable failure feedback, and review of accessibility observations. Completion counts alone do not establish learning effectiveness or public-deployment readiness.
